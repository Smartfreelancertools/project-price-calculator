How to Price a Freelance Project

A freelance project price should account for more than just the hours spent creating the work. Revisions, meetings, expenses, payment fees and your desired profit can all affect the final quote.

Why Use This Calculator?

  • Estimate your real project workload.
  • Include revision and communication time.
  • Account for project expenses.
  • Consider payment platform fees.
  • Build your desired profit into the quote.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I charge by the hour or by the project?
Both methods can work. A project quote can make your pricing easier for clients to understand while still being based on your estimated working time.

Should revisions be included in my price?
Yes. If revisions require your time, including them in the estimate can help prevent underpricing.

Can I use this calculator for different freelance jobs?
Yes. It can be used for design, writing, development, video editing, marketing and many other freelance services.
