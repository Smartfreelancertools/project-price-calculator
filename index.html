How to Price a Freelance Project

Pricing a freelance project is about more than multiplying your hourly rate by the number of hours you expect to work. A professional project quote should also consider revisions, client meetings, communication, project expenses, payment processing fees and the profit you want to earn.

This freelance project quote calculator combines these costs to help you create a more realistic fixed-price quote for your clients.

How This Freelance Pricing Calculator Works

First, enter your hourly rate and estimated main project hours. Then add the time you expect to spend on revisions and meetings or client communication. Include any additional project expenses, payment or platform fees, and your desired profit margin.

The calculator combines your working time and expenses and then calculates the amount you should quote for the project.

Why Include Revision and Meeting Time?

Freelancers often underestimate the time spent on revisions, client calls, messages and project communication. If these hours are not included in your estimate, your effective hourly earnings can be much lower than expected.

  • Include realistic project working hours.
  • Estimate revision time before sending your quote.
  • Include meetings and client communication.
  • Add software, materials or other project expenses.
  • Account for payment or freelance platform fees.
  • Include a profit margin that matches your business goals.

Hourly Rate vs. Project Pricing

Hourly pricing can be useful when the amount of work is uncertain. Project-based pricing can be easier for clients when the scope and deliverables are clearly defined. Even when you charge a fixed project price, calculating your expected hours can help you avoid underpricing your work.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I charge for a freelance project?
Your project price depends on your hourly rate, total working time, expenses, payment fees and desired profit. Use the calculator above to estimate a quote based on these factors.

Should revisions be included in my freelance quote?
Yes. If revisions require your time, estimate those hours before setting your project price. This can help reduce the risk of undercharging.

Should meetings and communication time be included?
Yes. Client meetings, calls, messages and project communication can take significant time, so including them can make your project estimate more realistic.

Can I use this calculator for different freelance services?
Yes. The calculator can be used for web development, graphic design, writing, video editing, marketing, SEO, consulting and many other freelance services.

Can I use this calculator for international clients?
Yes. The calculator uses your entered hourly rate and expenses, so it can be used when pricing projects for clients in different countries.
